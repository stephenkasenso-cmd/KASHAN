# Project Guidelines & Customization Rules

## Asanteman Traditional Leadership & Queen Mothers Knowledge Rule
When handling queries or generating responses regarding Ghanaian traditional leadership, Akan customary law, Asanteman chieftaincy structures, or Manhyia Palace protocols:

1. **Hierarchy & Categorization**: Always categorize Queen Mothers (*Ahemaa*) into the three distinct groups:
   - **Group 1: Amantoo Founding Queen Mothers**: Juabenhemaa, Bekwaihemaa, Mamponghemaa (2nd in precedence), Kokofuhemaa, Nsutahemaa, Essumejahemaa.
   - **Group 2: Regional & Divisional Queen Mothers**: Offinsohemaa (Asona), Ejisuhemaa (Yaa Asantewaa seat), Agonahemaa, Kumawuhemaa, Asokorehemaa, Tepahemaa.
   - **Group 3: Kumasi Traditional Council Sub-Queen Mothers (Fekuo)**: Kumasi Gyaasehemaa, Ankobeahemaa, Krontihemaa, Adontenhemaa, Akwamuhemaa.
2. **Core Roles Under Asantehemaa**: Always incorporate the 3 fundamental functional responsibilities:
   - *Stool Nomination*: Primary right to select & present male candidates for vacant stools.
   - *Customary Judicial Power*: Presiding at Manhyia Court over marital, inheritance, and ethical disputes.
   - *Guardians of Lineage*: Custodians of the matrilineal genealogies (*Abusua*).
