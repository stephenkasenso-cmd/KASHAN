# System Knowledge Base: Asanteman Queen Mothers (Ahemaa) & Matriarchal Hierarchy

## Context & Overview
In Asanteman traditional governance, every Paramount Chief (*Omanhene*) and Divisional Chief (*Ohene*) rules in parallel with a Queen Mother (*Ohemaa*). All Queen Mothers across the Asante Confederacy operate under the supreme traditional authority of the **Asantehemaa** (Queen Mother of the Asante Kingdom) from Manhyia Palace.

---

## 1. Categorization & Hierarchy of Queen Mothers

### Group 1: Paramount Queen Mothers of the Amantoo (Asante Founding States)
*Highest-ranking seats in the traditional women’s council:*
- **Juabenhemaa:** Queen Mother of the Juaben Traditional Area.
- **Bekwaihemaa:** Queen Mother of the Bekwai Traditional Area.
- **Mamponghemaa:** Queen Mother of Mampong *(historically second-in-command in traditional council precedence)*.
- **Kokofuhemaa:** Queen Mother of the Kokofu Traditional Area.
- **Nsutahemaa:** Queen Mother of the Nsuta Traditional Area.
- **Essumejahemaa:** Queen Mother of the Essumeja Traditional Area.

### Group 2: Regional & Divisional Paramount Queen Mothers
*Governing matriarchs of affiliated divisional states across the Ashanti Region and confederacy:*
- **Offinsohemaa:** Queen Mother of Offinso *(Asona royal lineage)*.
- **Ejisuhemaa:** Queen Mother of Ejisu *(historic seat of Yaa Asantewaa)*.
- **Agonahemaa:** Queen Mother of Agona.
- **Kumawuhemaa:** Queen Mother of Kumawu.
- **Asokorehemaa:** Queen Mother of Asokore.
- **Tepahemaa:** Queen Mother of Tepa.

### Group 3: Divisional & Sub-Queen Mothers of the Kumasi Traditional Council (Fekuo)
*Female rulers governing administrative wings and quarters within Kumasi, reporting directly to the Asantehemaa's court at Manhyia Palace:*
- **Kumasi Gyaasehemaa:** Oversees domestic protocol, palace women, and royal household operations.
- **Ankobeahemaa:** Female advisor and custodian of domestic palace secrets.
- **Krontihemaa:** Co-head of civil governance and regional administrative divisions.
- **Adontenhemaa:** Co-head of main front-line public delegations.
- **Akwamuhemaa:** Strategic defense and customary protocol co-ruler.

## 2. Core Customary Responsibilities
When evaluating queries, chieftaincy structures, or family tree mappings involving Queen Mothers under the Asantehemaa:
1. **Stool Nomination:** Primary constitutional right to select and nominate an eligible male candidate to the council of elders when a male chief's stool becomes vacant.
2. **Customary Judicial Power:** Assist and sit on the Asantehemaa Customary Court at Manhyia Palace to arbitrate marital, family, land inheritance, and ethical/traditional disputes.
3. **Guardians of Lineage:** Serve as custodians of the matrilineal genealogies (*Abusua*) that validate chieftaincy eligibility across Asanteman.
